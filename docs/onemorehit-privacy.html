Privacy Policy for ONE MORE HIT

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

ONE MORE HIT is a single-player baseball strategy game developed by BDN Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information We Collect

ONE MORE HIT does not require an account and does not collect personal information from players.

The game may store gameplay data locally on your device, such as save progress, settings, season results, league history, and preferences. This data is used only to provide gameplay features and remains on your device unless future cloud save features are added.

Internet Access

The game is designed to be playable without requiring a player account. If future versions add optional features such as cloud saves, purchases, analytics, or crash reporting, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those features.

In-App Purchases

ONE MORE HIT may offer optional in-app purchases. Payment processing is handled by the app store platform, such as Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Third-Party Services

ONE MORE HIT may use platform services provided by Google Play for app distribution, purchases, app integrity, or related store functionality. These services are governed by Google’s own privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

ONE MORE HIT is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided through the app, please contact us so we can address the issue.

Data Security

Since ONE MORE HIT does not require an account or collect personal information directly, the primary game data is stored locally on your device. You may remove local game data by uninstalling the app or using reset options provided in the game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
