Game Overview

Baseball Strategy With One More Decision

One More Hit turns baseball roster construction into a tense push-your-luck strategy game. Each draw adds talent and salary to the team. The challenge is knowing when to keep building and when to stand before the payroll becomes unsustainable.

Seasons are designed for quick mobile sessions, while persistent franchise history gives every championship, playoff run, record, and setback a place in the larger story of the dynasty.

Ready-to-Use Descriptions

25-word description

One More Hit is a push-your-luck baseball strategy game where every roster addition improves your team while bringing it closer to the salary cap.

50-word description

One More Hit is a fast-paced Android baseball strategy game built around roster decisions, salary-cap pressure, and push-your-luck draws. Build stronger teams, know when to stand, survive increasingly difficult seasons, and turn individual championship runs into a persistent franchise dynasty.

150-word description

One More Hit turns baseball roster construction into a fast, accessible push-your-luck strategy game for Android. Each new player adds ability to the roster, but also increases its salary. The central decision is simple to understand and difficult to master: draw again for a stronger team, or stand before an ambitious run becomes a bust.

Players progress through compact baseball seasons, face increasingly difficult opponents, chase rare and jackpot draws, and attempt to assemble a championship-caliber roster under pressure. Beyond each individual run, franchise history records championships, season results, milestones, and the developing legacy of the team.

Rather than controlling players on the field, One More Hit focuses on the tension behind building the roster. It is designed for players who enjoy baseball, card-based strategy, risk-versus-reward decisions, and sports-management progression in sessions suited to mobile play.
